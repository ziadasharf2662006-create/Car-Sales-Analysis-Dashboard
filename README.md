# 🚗 Interactive Car Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive Excel-based dashboard developed to analyze comprehensive car sales performance. The project uncovers key business metrics, including overall company performance, top-selling car models, regional distribution, and individual dealer performance to drive data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, Pivot Tables, Advanced Formulas, and Interactive Slicers.
* **Data Visualization:** Dynamic charts, KPIs, and professional dashboard design.

---

## 🔍 Key Objectives & Business Questions Answered
- What are the overall sales trends and total revenue generated?
- Which car models and brands are the top sellers in the market?
- How are individual dealerships performing against their targets?
- What are the peak sales periods across different timelines?

---

## 💡 Key Insights & Findings
- **Top Models:** Identified the top 3 car models contributing to over 40% of the total sales revenue.
- **Dealer Performance:** Highlighted top-performing dealerships and pinpointed branches requiring targeted marketing or sales support.
- **Sales Trends:** Discovered seasonal peaks in car purchases, enabling better inventory forecasting.

## 📁 Repository Structure
- `data/`: Contains the raw and cleaned datasets used for the analysis.
- `dashboard/`: The final interactive Excel file (`.xlsx`).
- `assets/`: Images and screenshots used for documentation.

---

## 🚀 How to View the Project
1. Go to the `dashboard/` folder.
2. Download the Excel file or open it directly.
3. Use the interactive Slicers in Excel to filter data by region, car model, or dealer.
